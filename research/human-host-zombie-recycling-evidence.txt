D. Reproduction and fix scope
Serialized NPC_Spawner_Mgr._maxDeadRagdolls=4 in generated catalog objects level1#762 and bundles/icons_common_scenes_all.bundle::serialized-1#11776.
An isolated C# reproduction uses the unchanged decompiled NPC_Input.On_Char_Died method and the initial overflow branch of NPC_Spawner_Mgr.Delay_Back_Dead_NPC_To_Pool.
Engine coroutine scheduling, pool reset, and retained-body storage are boundary substitutes. This is not a clean-vanilla gameplay reproduction.
Starting with no pending bodies: 12 same-frame deaths produced 8 disabled pooled objects for retained-body limits 50, 300 and 1000. Four same-frame deaths produced 0; five produced 1.
The new scope guard reduced these failures to 0 in the same isolated reproduction.
Separate tests execute the production prefix, pool postfix and finalizer against engine boundary substitutes: 48 assertions passed, including no-op repeats and already-correct upstream ordering.
Local Expanded Hordes 0.2.1 source commit: 8825782db9554ecfffb91f71b99a222d30fb4c08 (not published).
Relevant mod methods: HordeDeathRecycle.Prefix/Finalizer, HordePoolReturn.Postfix/Ready, DeathRecycleGuard<T>.
Gameplay verification of the preventative guard is pending. The separate recovery workaround was play-tested; the user reported no further flyers.
Per-burst weapon attribution remains unknown. No multiplayer or clean-vanilla gameplay test was run.
